# Model experiments and final test conclusion

[中文](MODEL_EXPERIMENTSCN.md) · [Data treatment and coverage](DATA_IMPROVEMENT.md) · [Documentation index](README.md)

**Final course choice: retain the original V4 60/20/20 six-MLP checkpoint unchanged.** This is an offline academic reconstruction of the Red Deer gauge-derived stage/depth proxy. It is not a validated forecast or physical depth measurement. **Do not use V4 or any experimental output for real flood warnings, emergency response, evacuation, or operations.** No experimental model or guard is promoted.

## Baseline and evaluation labels

- The original V4 fits terminal 05CC002 proxy depth by MSE. Its 12 inputs are three routed discharges plus nine 1/6/24-hour rainfall features evaluated on three source clocks. P01–P05 have no independent observed training labels. The split uses a seven-day embargo on both sides of boundaries and preserves event groups defined by terminal Q ≥100 m³/s, with 72-hour merging and padding. Q is for split design and scoring, not a prediction feature.
- **Original reverse-time V4 test:** 146,319 eligible View rows; 6,568 excluded for the embargo, leaving 85,777 newest train, 26,718 validation and 27,256 oldest test rows (May 5–August 11, 2025 UTC). Validation selected epoch 1; 16 epochs ran. Test MAE/RMSE/bias: **10.31/13.92/+6.17 cm**. The 80/20 variant used the same 27,256 formal test rows (MAE 9.68 cm), but the largest June crest underprediction was −19.23 cm versus −9.21 cm in 60/20/20. Both June 2026 events are in original V4 training. Historical View alignment used terminal information; chronology and an embargo do not remove that dependency. [Split and training records](../data/processed/segment_six_mlp_matched_early_stop_20261118T0108Z/v4_retrain/preflight.json) · [Original results](../data/processed/segment_six_mlp_matched_early_stop_20261118T0108Z/v4_retrain/training_report.json)
- **Later 2025 replay:** the June and July cases were already inspected. Some later variants were frozen on 2026 data before replaying 2025, but that does not make 2025 a fresh, independent event-level or prospective test. **Later 2026 checks:** E1/E2 blocked studies hold out whole events with a seven-day purge but may use later data; strict-forward E2 train ends before June 17 04:45 UTC and evaluation starts June 24 04:45 UTC. These are two events, not three. Some checks still use the historical View; later raw matching removes that View from the experiment but cannot verify historical publication times.

**Read the numbers:** signed error/bias = estimate − observation. Crest error is evaluated at the observed crest timestamp; peak timing compares the timestamp of the predicted maximum to the observed crest within the stated window (+ means late). It is not warning lead. A model can predict the highest value on the wrong wave; its max-to-max difference is not its error at the real crest. Routine is terminal Q 20–40 m³/s inclusive; extreme is Q ≥140 m³/s. These are evaluation strata, not official thresholds.

## What was tried and what it ruled out

1. **Route selection, numerical targets, and early model families.** The [frozen 27-route workflow](frozen-route-experiment.md) constrained matching on training-only route selection; the [non-saturating v2 trial](tail-model-experiment.md) tried raw inputs, residual six blocks, positive output and greater high-flow loss weight. On 35,306 identical targets, Q MAE 6.02 → 6.14 m³/s, and all 3,161 Q≥100 rows were underestimated by v2. [Depth-weighted run](../data/processed/segment_nn_weather_depth_weighted_v1_20261117/training_report.json) and [earlier routing-depth run](../data/processed/segment_six_mlp_routing_depth_20261118T0045Z/training_report.json) are distinct experiments, not interchangeable with final V4. The later 80-trial V4-family search compared widths, activations, dropout, optimizer settings and loss/weights on blocked development. The winning trial reduced pooled fixed-2025 MAE 10.31 → 8.02 cm but worsened Q≥140 MAE 22.29 → 32.33 cm and the largest crest error −9.21 → −41.73 cm. Its routine denominator is 11,745 (the search report’s label join), not 11,779. [Tuning ledger summary](../data/processed/segment_six_mlp_matched_early_stop_20261118T0108Z/v4_retrain/tuning_20261118/final.json).
2. **Continuous postprocessing and Dickson guard.** The continuous search evaluated 40,464 distinct recipes across nine calibration, bias, learned residual, short-history and transport-related families, using E1/E2 blocked and strict-forward checks. Five passed development conditions; the locked winner still failed to recover June/July timing on historical replay. The separate 346 control/recipe evaluations of the opt-in Dickson guard constrained just the modeled current-D discontinuity (5 cm retained initially, 30-min half-life, 120-min cutoff). At switch pairs it reduced ≥10 cm jumps 174 → 2 (n=980), not storm timing. [Continuous search audit](../data/processed/segment_six_mlp_matched_early_stop_20261118T0108Z/v4_retrain/depth_continuous_20261119T1630Z/ledger_audit.json) · [Guard protocol and controls](../data/processed/segment_six_mlp_matched_early_stop_20261118T0108Z/v4_retrain/dickson_contribution_20261119/README.md).
3. **Why shifting or dampening alone did not solve the rise.** For guarded V4, the June true-crest to first predicted local crest interval contains 131 rows unchanged by the guard; the first local maximum is +10h50, not +57h20. A fixed-network substitution attributes +18.62 cm of the +18.67 cm modeled increase to rain-pattern changes, not physical causation. Wet/dry routine bias was +15.27/+6.96 cm (6,741/5,038 rows), so rain is not the only source of routine bias. Historical oracle reindexing wanted June 14h earlier but July 22h30 later; no common lag fix. Guarded V4’s modeled June +12h rise was +0.35 vs +12.60 cm observed. A constrained sustained-flow term (A96, selected from 288 rules plus identity and 3 fitted residuals) reduced June +10 cm lag 45h40 → 21h30 but barely changed +12h depth rise (0.35 → 1.38 cm), slightly worsened routine MAE (11.85 → 11.92 cm), and added two ≥10 cm jumps (27 → 29/26,786). Its conditional monotonic correction does not make V4 globally monotone. [Local/wide crest and routine diagnosis](../data/processed/segment_six_mlp_matched_early_stop_20261118T0108Z/v4_retrain/guard_diagnosis_20261119/README.md) · [Flow/rain response](../data/processed/segment_six_mlp_matched_early_stop_20261118T0108Z/v4_retrain/guard_rise_response_20261120/README.md) · [Travel-time sensitivity](../data/processed/segment_six_mlp_matched_early_stop_20261118T0108Z/v4_retrain/travel_time_20261120/README.md) · [Sustained rise trial](../data/processed/segment_six_mlp_matched_early_stop_20261118T0108Z/v4_retrain/monotone_rise_20261120/evaluation_historical.json).
4. **Direct rise versus changes in reconstructed stages.** The direct experiments predict observed H(t+h)−H(t) using inputs matched before issuance with a simulated publication delay. The later MLP study instead subtracts two reconstructed stages; its 1h/3h differences are diagnostics, not as-issued forecasts. On its strict-forward E2 1h common rows, neither matching arm hit any +5 cm rise (32 covered of 45 known; n=35,529); 1,013 → 935 false-positive rows. Full support 3h nowcast hits 30 → 53 of 288 known, but false-positive rows 2,216 → 2,464 on expanded support, not a paired alert improvement. The comparator using terminal stage from 30 minutes earlier uses information the upstream-only neural models do not receive. Direct-forecast coverage and false-alert counts are documented separately in [Data treatment and coverage](DATA_IMPROVEMENT.md).

The reported measurements and metrics are local readouts, not an export of the raw datasets or a reproducible pipeline delivery. Final-test success here means documenting the experiment and its limits, not establishing forecast skill sufficient for operations.
