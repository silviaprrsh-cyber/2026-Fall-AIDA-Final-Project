# Limitations and next evidence

Gauge-zero/vertical-datum transfer is unresolved; experimental stage is not true depth. In response to a student datum inquiry submitted on 2026-11-10, Environment and Climate Change Canada (National Hydrological Services – Alberta, 2026-11-17) provided 10-year Benchmark History Reports and confirmed that most stations operate in an **assumed datum**, that stage records may not be contiguous across gauge moves, and that datum conversion factors may not exist or apply across the entire record. This official confirmation directly supports keeping station stage separate from CGVD28 surveyed cross-section elevations and true water depth. Upstream route assumptions can select very old Medicine inputs and discontinuous timestamps; their physical transit interpretation is unvalidated. The peak is theoretically reachable under current output bounds but remains poorly fitted. Test periods have been inspected repeatedly, observations are dependent, and route-exposed internal validation is not independent evidence. Q100/Q140 slice counts are small relative to all rows. Persistence uses current endpoint information absent from the network.

The [formal EDA](app-or-report/final-eda-report.md) completed approval and lake-coverage sensitivities using seven-day block uncertainty where estimable. It has not validated independent flood events, causal effects, operational travel times or surveyed depth. The complete-lake cohort omits substantial periods, and approval status limits comparability without showing the data are invalid. A verified station-by-station vertical transfer, independent event list and untouched observations are needed for stronger claims. The final uploadable clean package, any D2L-supplied validator, genuine peer feedback and in-class defence remain pending.

## Package limits

Live observations do not make the [packaged prototype](README.md) an operational forecast. The package keeps the V4 weights but replaces the historical arrival-input construction with a source-only adapter. The original scores do not validate it. The +3 h and +9 h calculations need already-received upstream inputs, and can abstain. Never substitute a modeled input for a missing observation or use this project for actual warning, emergency or safety decisions. A cloud TLS/availability trial and independent untouched event evaluation are distinct tasks, not results established by the local demo.
