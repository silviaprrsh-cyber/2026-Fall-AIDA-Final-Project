# Data provenance and identity

This record distinguishes external observations, existing project derivatives and analysis outputs. It covers the AIDA 2156 final EDA and the separate offline raw-source audit. It does not authorize release of all local project sources.

## Final hydrology analysis

- **Authority:** Water Survey of Canada (WSC), Environment and Climate Change Canada (ECCC), [Water Office unit values](https://wateroffice.ec.gc.ca/services/real_time_data/csv/inline). The source acquisition batch is `20261103T070414Z`, stored locally under `data/sources/eccc_wateroffice/raw/unit_values/20261103T070414Z/`. Raw CSV and individual acquisition receipts retain the query, archive UTC receipt, status, byte count and SHA-256. The receipt does **not** prove the first public availability of each historical observation.
- **Exact working inputs:** five existing ECCC-derived files, `data/processed/model_unit_history/20261103T070414Z/<station>_observations.csv`, for 05CB007, 05CB001, 05CC007, 05CC003 and 05CC002. These are frozen paired station-observation exports with a station/UTC key. The final analysis consumes existing observations and quality fields; it does not query PostgreSQL, download, rerun ETL, fill missing observations, convert stage to depth or read V4 predictions. The [input checksum ledger](eda-input-checksums.csv) describes the working inputs. Source: [Water Office real-time hydrometric data](https://wateroffice.ec.gc.ca/mainmenu/real_time_data_index_e.html). See the [provider disclaimer](https://wateroffice.ec.gc.ca/disclaimer_info_e.html) and [FAQ](https://wateroffice.ec.gc.ca/contactus/faq_e.html). Real-time values can be provisional and revised; attribution does not imply endorsement.
- **Analysis vs. release:** original receiver clocks, source path columns, raw CSVs, retrieval sidecars, other source fields and model assets are outside the minimal analytical projection. Refer to the final EDA report and the machine-readable [dictionary](data-dictionary.csv) for complete-case quality definitions and the distinction between a lake elevation and a river gauge stage.
- **Comparability:** the offline raw hydrometry audit additionally uses acquisition batch `20261111T030808Z`. Later duplicate records are kept by file/dated acquisition order and overlapping revisions are counted. The final EDA stays on the older frozen input and cannot silently import those later revisions. Neither archived dataset is proof of what a historical operator knew at the time.

## Byte identity and verification

[eda-input-checksums.csv](eda-input-checksums.csv) records repository-relative paths, byte counts and SHA-256 per inventoried file. It covers the five frozen formal inputs, Water Office CSVs in both acquisition batches for the five selected stations, four archived monthly climate snapshots, the CV evidence inputs and four sampled GeoTIFFs, and the GIS databases by member file (excluding transient locks), provider reports and derived scope inputs. It does not claim to hash every one of the 3,704 imagery files or all files ever used by the project. A SHA-256 identifies saved bytes, not approval, correctness or publication time. All 190 Water Office CSVs and 97 climate GeoJSON pages matched the SHA-256 in their saved response sidecars. The five frozen local hashes also matched the prior provenance record. Other rows have no response-sidecar comparison and are marked `not_applicable`.

For a local input verification, run `Get-FileHash -Algorithm SHA256 -LiteralPath '<repository-relative path>'` from the repository root and compare the recorded row. The [raw EDA evidence guide](../output/raw_data_eda/README.md) documents authorized local dependencies and reproduction. Weather pages, provider receipts, raw TIFFs and Alberta geodatabases are reference-only and not bundled in the clean AIDA 2156 package.
