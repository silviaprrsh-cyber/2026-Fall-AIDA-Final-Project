# Original-source audits: what the other evidence can establish

This is contextual evidence for the historical Dickson–05CC002 decision. The audit code and full tables are in the working repository under `analysis/raw_*audit.py` and `output/raw_data_eda/`. These independently audited source archives are not dependencies of the final hydrometric analysis and are not included in the clean ZIP. The table below provides static, accessible equivalents of the key audit counts.

| Source | Observed in the audit | Consequence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hydrometric source archives | Dickson recorded zero measured zero-flow values in the audited archive. Red Deer recorded 155,657 stage values and 2.94% missing slots in its observed span. Sylvan Lake recorded 105,790 stage values and 32.85% missing in its observed span; its longest contiguous gap was about 179 days. | Dickson missing-as-zero in V4 is an assumption, not direct evidence of gate closure. This EDA does not substitute zeros for missing observations. The raw in-span denominators differ from the final common UTC window. |
| Red Deer airport precipitation | 67 missing hourly slots: 33 absent records and 34 records with blank precipitation. 12,730 hours were measured zero. | Missing rain is not dry weather. Rainfall is not used to adjust the final bivariate river comparison, so it may explain some association. |
| Sentinel-2 optical imagery | 3,704 local raster files (955 historical, 2,749 gap-fill) on 348 sensing days; multiple grids per day. The historical corridor quality screen retained 285 of 775 raster files (36.8%), zero in winter. Median clear area: 94.72% for the full corridor versus 74.64% for the river core. | Whole-corridor clarity overstates clarity where width is measured. Screening eligibility is not label approval; this archive cannot independently validate width or depth. |
| Cross-section GIS and field surveys | 229 scoped lines link to source features: 38 surveyed Red Deer lines link directly to 5,268 points by original section ID; 191 County/Markerville lines are planned and use only spatial candidate association. The 229 lines include 146 mainstem and 83 tributary lines. No scoped line is marked profile- or bank-elevation-verified. | A valid spatial/database link is not a validated hydraulic profile. The CGVD28 survey elevations and station-relative gauge stage cannot be subtracted without a verified vertical transfer. P01–P05 have no independent observed depth. |

These counts describe the audited archives, not independent sample sizes or new validation. The ordinary and stricter observational samples and their dependence intervals appear in the [decision report](final-eda-report.md). The design does not infer hydraulic travel time, surveyed depth, an inundation boundary or real warning performance.
