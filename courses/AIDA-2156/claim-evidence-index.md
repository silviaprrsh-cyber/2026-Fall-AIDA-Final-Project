# Claim-to-evidence index

The current [formal index](claim-evidence-index.csv) maps P1–P5 to fields, integrity checks, output, uncertainty, sensitivity, limitations and release status. See the [decision report](app-or-report/final-eda-report.md) for prose and tables.

- **P1:** Observational coverage differs by source, and high-flow values are not described by a typical-flow mean alone.
- **P2:** Lagged correlations show descriptive covariation, not verified transit times.
- **P3:** The final-only rule removes all joint lake-complete timestamps; the difference in joint means is not estimable.
- **P4:** Lake completeness selects historical dates. The modest same-date mean difference does not describe omitted periods or establish a causal lake effect.
- **P5:** Gauge readings, survey elevations and experimental depths are not interchangeable. Withhold operational use.

E1/E2, the [historical ML claim index](output/model-claim-evidence-index.csv), is retained separately. It does not replace the formal EDA.
