# GIS data

Survey, river-network, coordinate-grid and landmark sources are separated under `sources/`. Working layers and projects are under `processed/`; spatial checks are under `reports/`. Keep source CRS, units, dates and permission records. Water/weather series belong to AIDA 2156 and are explicitly referenced. Inputs remain local and ignored by Git.
