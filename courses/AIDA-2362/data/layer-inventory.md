# Current QGIS layer inventory and authority

The [layer inventory](layer-inventory.csv) lists the eight local vector layers and one optional OSM basemap for the current Dickson–05CC002 project. Counts and file hashes for the active layers are in the [working manifest](../project/manifest.json); its older historical inputs are source lineage, not a final package list. See [the current QGIS audit](../outputs/current-qgis-validation.json) for every layer's actual observed field and geometry statistics.

## Source evidence

- **River network.** [NRCan National Hydro Network](https://open.canada.ca/data/en/dataset/a4b190fe-e090-4e6d-881e-b87956c07977), published under [Open Government Licence – Canada](https://open.canada.ca/en/open-government-licence-canada). The local archived NRCan 05cc000 receipt is 2026-09-28 UTC, with archive timestamp 2019-07-24; receipt and archive metadata are not survey dates. Contains information licensed under the Open Government Licence – Canada. Project route clipping and landmark geometry are derived work and not official determinations.
- **Hydrometry and climate.** ECCC [Hydrometric Monitoring Stations](https://open.canada.ca/data/en/dataset/46763060-e859-4812-8da5-2361d99b4c34), [Water Office information](https://open.canada.ca/data/en/dataset/0b30719a-f7a6-4f3c-ae37-000acfe9f5a3), [MSC GeoMet Climate Stations](https://api.weather.gc.ca/collections/climate-stations?f=html) and [Hourly Climate Observations](https://open.canada.ca/data/en/dataset/df2e6e1a-6057-4c4d-a509-94aa57705a8c). The federal portal lists open licences for those catalogue records. GeoMet links the [ECCC Data Servers End-use Licence](https://eccc-msc.github.io/open-data/licence/readme_en/), which allows reuse with attribution subject to its terms and exceptions. Attribution: Environment and Climate Change Canada, Meteorological Service of Canada and Water Survey of Canada.
- **Survey material.** The locally retained [survey review](reports/manual_data_review.md) records the original files and survey periods. On 2026-09-30, Alberta Environment and Protected Areas (EPA) provided the four flood-study survey geodatabases and reports for non-commercial academic use at Red Deer Polytechnic. On **2026-11-23**, Alberta EPA confirmed in writing that the provided Red Deer River survey data is publicly available information and that EPA has no concerns with using the clipped derived cross-section layers in the course submission as long as **Alberta Environment and Protected Areas (EPA)** is credited. Attribution: *Cross-section survey line derivatives derived from flood-study survey datasets provided by Alberta Environment and Protected Areas (EPA).* Private email correspondence (`Protected A`) and original full geodatabases remain excluded from Git and the D2L submission ZIP.
- **OpenStreetMap.** © OpenStreetMap contributors; [attribution](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright) and [OSM raster tile usage policy](https://operations.osmfoundation.org/policies/tiles/). No tile cache, bulk archive or new basemap requests are part of the local technical check. The existing PDF carries attribution. For offline review, use the eight local vector layers, table and already-exported map where permitted; do not imply server tiles are bundled.

## Scale, coordinates and time

The report is for regional review and the [map PDF](../outputs/Overhead%20view.pdf) uses its printed 0–20 km scale bar. It is not a parcel- or structure-level measurement map. Geographic vectors store longitude/latitude in EPSG:4326; the display uses EPSG:3857; route distance uses EPSG:3400 in metres. Survey inputs in EPSG:3780 and the CGVD28 elevation reference are distinct from the mapped display and do not establish comparability to gauge zero; on 2026-11-17, ECCC (NHS Hydrological AB) confirmed in response to a student datum inquiry that most hydrometric stations operate in an assumed datum and that conversion factors may not exist or apply across gauge moves. The full 2017/2022 survey periods are not contemporaneous observations, and the station archive availability snapshot does not represent present monitoring.
