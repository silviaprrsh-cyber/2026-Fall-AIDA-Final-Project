# Historical review of the first 24 new candidate previews

The first 24 thumbnails in the new automated-candidate contact sheet were visually inspected. Images 8, 9 and 13 showed whitening or haze-like features and were initially held for native-resolution checking; the other 21 could receive priority review/annotation. This did not identify a cloud type, establish independent truth or approve any image for training. Image identities, hashes, positions and reasons are in visual_review_first24.json. The remaining new candidates had not been visually inspected at this checkpoint.

The later [current review](CURRENT_REVIEW.md) retains these three images as challenging-region cases rather than excluding the whole images.
