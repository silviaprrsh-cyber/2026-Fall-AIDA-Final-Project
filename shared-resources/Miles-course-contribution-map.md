### How the Three Contributions Connect (While Remaining Separately Assessed)

- **Shared geographic backbone (`AIDA 2362 → 2154 & 2156`):** `AIDA 2362` defines the verified `56.0 km` reach, nine target coordinates (`P01–P05` + 4 river gauges), and 229 cross-section locations. `AIDA 2154` extracts native 10 m Sentinel-2 crops centered on those exact nine GIS points; `AIDA 2156` audits the 18-month ECCC time series for those four river gauges plus Sylvan Lake `05CC003`.
- **Strict scientific firewall (`AIDA 2154 ↛ 2156`):** Because `AIDA 2154` proved that 10 m satellite segmentation fails on narrow channels and trails a static baseline under blind review, **zero CV widths or masks are fed into `AIDA 2156`**. Similarly, `AIDA 2156`'s formal observation study is kept strictly separate from contextual V4 hydrological experiments, and **no course claims operational flood-warning readiness**.
