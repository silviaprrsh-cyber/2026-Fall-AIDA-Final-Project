## 1. Problem, Intended User, and Decision

- **Current situation:** Along the ~56.0 km mainstem of the Red Deer River between Dickson Dam outlet (`05CB007`) and Red Deer gauge `05CC002` (with tributary inflows from Little Red Deer River `05CB001` and Medicine River `05CC007`, plus Sylvan Lake `05CC003` as a separate reference), public hydrometric gauges, government cross-section surveys, and 10 m Sentinel-2 satellite scenes exist in separate formats with uneven coverage, provisional approval flags, unverified gauge-zero datums, and mixed-pixel spatial limits.
- **Intended user:** A hypothetical municipal river-infrastructure reviewer (not "everyone") evaluating historical environmental monitoring evidence.
- **Decision supported:** Determine which historical hydrometric stations, spatial cross-section associations, and satellite-derived water observations are trustworthy enough to support further technical investigation along `Dickson Dam → 05CC002` (`P01–P05`), and where outputs must be **limited or withheld** rather than fed into downstream modeling or safety decisions.
- **Why it matters:** Treating provisional records, assumed-datum gauge stages, schematic weather zones, or unvalidated 10 m satellite widths as operational truth would silently corrupt hydrological modeling or mislead municipal risk screening.

## 2. Proposed Solution, Data Authority, Scope, and Success Criterion

- **Proposed solution:** A connected, three-course reproducible evidence workflow:
  1. **AIDA 2154 (CV):** Evaluate whether 10 m Sentinel-2 imagery can supply reliable point-width evidence across nine GIS sites (`05CB001`, `05CB007`, `05CC002`, `05CC007`, `P01–P05`) using a leak-free split, static training-mask baseline, CPU CNN, indirect hydrometric width checks, and a strictly separate 9-site blind manual audit.
  2. **AIDA 2156 (EDA):** Conduct a reproducible standard-library historical evidence-adequacy study on 18 months of frozen 5-minute ECCC observations (`2025-05-03` to `2026-11-03 UTC`), separating missing/rejected/off-grid units, computing 7-day block uncertainty intervals, and testing two consequential sensitivities (`P-S1` strict Final filter; `P-S2` Sylvan Lake inclusion), kept strictly separate from contextual V4 model experiments.
  3. **AIDA 2362 (GIS):** Deliver a portable, relative-path QGIS project (`EPSG:3400` metric analysis) mapping the verified 56.0 km reach, `P01–P05`, gauges, and 229 cross-sections, testing a `<= 25 m` vs. `<= 50 m` along-river proximity sensitivity and providing an accessible 246-feature map/table equivalent.
- **Data sources & authority:** ECCC Wateroffice & MSC GeoMet (public open data; ECCC confirmed assumed-datum constraints in writing on `2026-11-17`), NRCan hydrography (public open data), Government of Alberta EPA Red Deer River flood surveys (written attribution clearance received `2026-11-23`; `Protected A` correspondence and raw GDBs excluded), and Copernicus Sentinel-2 via CDSE (open Copernicus data, tokens excluded). Full API/DB details are in [`Miles-data-sources.md`](Miles-data-sources.md).
- **Scope & exclusions:**
  - **In scope:** Historical `Dickson Dam → 05CC002` (~56.0 km), `P01–P05`, unfrozen/archived periods, reproducible offline replay, honest negative-result preservation.
  - **Out of scope:** Downstream `P06–P11` (removed `2026-11-04` due to lack of comparable downstream validation gauge), operational flood warning or emergency response, physical water-depth equivalence from assumed-datum gauge stages, and deployment of CV widths into hydrological models.
- **Observable success criterion:** An independent reviewer can (a) replay each course's artifact cleanly on permitted inputs (`python -B -E -S` for 2156, CPU replay + 14 tests for 2154, clean-extracted QGIS 3.44.14 project for 2362), (b) trace every quantitative claim to a frozen output, baseline/sensitivity comparison, and explicit uncertainty/failure check, and (c) verify that each recommendation respects its documented evidence boundary.
