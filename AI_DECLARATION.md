# AI Use Declaration

Project: Red Deer River Near-Real-Time Monitoring and Potential Overbank-Risk Assessment

I used OpenAI ChatGPT and Codex to assist with this project. AI assistance included substantive drafting, code generation and revision, and technical design suggestions. Its involvement covered:

- Developing and refining the project scope, methodology, proposal and technical documentation; translating between English and Chinese and formatting documents.
- Assisting with data-source discovery, API access design, station and cross-section association methods, observation-time processing, precipitation aggregation and simplified hydraulic calculation approaches.
- Generating and modifying Python code for data acquisition, local processing and supporting utilities; assisting with debugging, test creation and execution, and interpretation of outputs.
- Organizing project files and documentation, maintaining working instructions and identifying assumptions, data gaps and implementation limitations.

I defined and revised the project objectives, provided requirements and feedback, selected the scope of authorized work, and supplied manually downloaded government survey packages. AI suggestions influenced the implementation and documentation. I am responsible for deciding what to retain, understanding and explaining the submitted work, checking its sources and results, and complying with the applicable course requirements and data-use conditions.

Project observations and survey measurements are attributed to their original providers. AI-generated explanations, code, assumptions and illustrative examples are not observational evidence. Software test results do not establish the accuracy of hydraulic estimates. Unverified station-to-section relationships, elevation references and risk calculations must remain identified as limitations. This declaration does not claim that all AI-assisted code or analytical results have been independently validated.

This declaration describes assistance used to date. CV model training, joint feedback, dashboards and deployment remain planned work and are not represented here as completed. The declaration should be updated when the actual use of AI or the submitted deliverables change. Disclosure of AI use does not itself establish instructor approval of that use or approval to reuse work across courses.
